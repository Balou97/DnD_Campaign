# Alden
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Alden is a young half-elf whose bright, curious eyes gleam like morning dew on polished silver. His hair, a tousled mix of chestnut brown and sun-kissed gold, falls freely around his pointed ears — a subtle mark of his elven heritage. He dresses in finely tailored yet comfortable clothes: soft linen shirts with embroidered cuffs, a fitted vest of deep sapphire blue, and well-worn leather boots that speak of long journeys on dusty roads. A delicate silver brooch shaped like a lightning bolt pins his cloak at the shoulder — a symbol of the Thunderblades. Slung across his back or resting in his hands is his prized violin, its polished wood gleaming as though charged with faint magic, the strings humming softly with every brush of his fingers.
### 1.2. Personality
Alden’s spirit is as lively as the songs he plays. Bursting with enthusiasm and wonder, he treats every moment as if it were a scene from a grand adventure — even a shared meal or a well-timed joke becomes a tale worth retelling. His optimism is infectious, lifting the hearts of his companions after even the hardest battles. Though young and sometimes naive, Alden possesses a deep love for storytelling and music, channeling both into his bardic craft. He is determined to immortalize every triumph, however small, in song — ensuring that no deed, no matter how fleeting, ever fades into silence.
## 2. Biography
### 2.1. Background
Alden is a young and fresh Adventurer of the Thunderblades. Together with [[Arvid]] and [[Toruk]] he helped slaying [[Gorthok the Thunder Boar.jpg| Gorthok the Thunder Boar]] near [[Amphail]]. Despite this victory they lost their Companion Lyra when 
they fought against the Frost wolves. After this quest they headed north towards [[Steelheim]], where they rest and get ready for their next adventure.
