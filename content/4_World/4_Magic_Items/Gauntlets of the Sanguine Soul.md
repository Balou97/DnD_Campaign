# `=this.file.name`
## Profile

**Description:** 
The **Gauntlets of Sanguine Soul** are forged from dark, burnished steel veined with thin channels resembling crimson capillaries. These grooves faintly glow with a deep red light whenever their wearer calls upon their Crimson Rite, as though the gauntlets themselves thirst for battle. The knuckles are reinforced with jagged, fang-like ridges designed to tear as much as they strike, and dried, rune-like etchings trace along the fingers and wrists—ancient hemocraft sigils that pulse with each heartbeat. When a powerful blow lands, the crimson veins briefly flare brighter, drinking in the spilled life force before fading back to a slow, hungry glow.

## Powers:
**Soul Strike.**  
When you make an unarmed strike while your Crimson Rite is active, you roll one additional Hemocraft die of rite damage.

**Hungering Defense.**  
While wearing these gauntlets, you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

**Life Theft.**  
When you deal damage to a creature with your Crimson Rite using an unarmed strike, you can choose to regain hit points equal to half the total damage dealt (rounded up).

- **Recharge.** Once you use this property, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest, unless you trigger a Violent Release.
    
- **Violent Release.** Whenever you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike while your Crimson Rite is active, you regain the use of this property immediately.
