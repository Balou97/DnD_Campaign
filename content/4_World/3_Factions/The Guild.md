# The Guild
## Profile

**Description:** 
The Guild is a secretive and formidable bounty hunter organization, composed mostly of seasoned outlaws, mercenaries, and bounty hunters who have earned their reputation through years of dangerous work. Operating under an iron-clad code of honor, the members take on jobs that range from capturing fugitives to hunting down powerful targets for the highest bidder. However, despite their vast network and influence, very little is known about the true identity of their leader, and the members go to great lengths to keep this secret hidden. Their loyalty lies to the Guild itself, and their enigmatic leader remains a mystery even within their own ranks.

The Guild's base of operations is a small, weather-worn ruin in the frozen north called **Frosthold Keep**. Located atop a jagged cliff, surrounded by windswept tundra, the keep is a place of solitude and secrecy, its crumbling walls providing shelter and a sense of hidden power. The location is known only to those who have earned the Guild's trust, and the cold, desolate landscape serves as a fitting backdrop for the Guild's brutal way of life. Inside, hardened bounty hunters gather, exchanging information, preparing for missions, and living by their code—“This is the Way.” It is an oath that ensures that members honor their commitments, never betray one another, and always face challenges head-on, no matter the outcome. They do not steal from one another, and every bounty is shared with respect for the work and the risk involved. The code is the Guild's backbone, and it has kept them strong and united for years.
