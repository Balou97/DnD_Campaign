# Aerenthal
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Aerenthal appears largely human at first glance, though subtle traits reveal his celestial heritage. His skin is lightly tanned, and faint silver lines—resembling delicate runes—trace across parts of his face and body. These markings softly glow whenever he channels arcane power, a quiet reminder of the celestial blood that runs through him.

He wears his long brown hair loose around his shoulders and keeps a full brown beard that frames his calm, thoughtful expression. His equipment reflects both elegance and practicality: finely crafted light armor with arcane runes etched into the bracers, designed to aid his spellcasting. A deep blue cloak drapes across his shoulders, often flowing with his movements in battle. At his side he carries a beautifully balanced sword, which serves not only as his weapon but also as a conduit for his magic, allowing him to weave spells directly through his blade.
### 1.2. Personality
Aerenthal is disciplined, reflective, and deeply mindful of the consequences of his actions. The accident that changed his life left him with a strong sense of responsibility, and he approaches both magic and combat with caution and control. He avoids reckless displays of power and prefers precise, calculated actions that minimize unnecessary harm.

Despite the guilt he carries, Aerenthal remains compassionate and determined to do good. He believes strongly in redemption and strives to use his abilities to protect those who cannot protect themselves. His calm demeanor often hides the intensity of his inner struggle, but those who travel with him will find a loyal ally who holds himself to high moral standards and seeks constantly to prove that power can be guided by wisdom.
## 2. Biography
### 2.1. Background
Aerenthal was born under what many believed to be a rare celestial alignment, marking him as an Aasimar touched by divine influence. From an early age he was visited in dreams by Lumira, a celestial guide who spoke of a greater destiny awaiting him. As his magical abilities began to manifest, he was admitted to a prestigious arcane academy, [[The Mystic Conclave of the Arcane]], where his natural talent quickly distinguished him from his peers. Specializing in evocation and abjuration, he showed exceptional control over destructive combat magic.

Everything changed during a practical examination in his fifth year. During a staged battle against summoned elementals, Aerenthal unleashed a carefully aimed fireball to protect his fellow students—unaware that one of them, a halfling illusionist named Miren, was hidden nearby under invisibility. The explosion severely injured her, leaving her blind and without her hands, ending her ability to practice magic forever. Though the academy deemed it a tragic accident, Aerenthal could not forgive himself. He left the academy and swore never again to cast spells meant to directly harm another living being. Seeking a new path, he trained with the Bladesingers, learning to combine magic with swordplay and to use his power for protection rather than destruction. Now he travels as an adventurer, striving to redeem himself and hoping that one day he might find a way to restore what Miren lost.

## 4. Appearances
Aerenthal was praying quietly in the Temple of Selûne in [[Waterdeep]], seeking guidance from Lumira. During his prayer, [[FEND]] entered the temple in search of capable adventurers who might assist him with a task, leading to their first encounter.
## 5. Relationships
- Miren: Former fellow student at the arcane academy. Severely injured in the accident caused by Aerenthal’s fireball; he still feels deep guilt and sends her money whenever he can.
- Lumira: Aerenthal’s celestial guide, a champion of Selûne, who has visited him in dreams since childhood—once speaking of destiny and now of redemption.
- [[Alatar]]: Headmaster of the Mystic Conclave of the Arcane and one of Aerenthal’s former teachers; a powerful wizard who recognized his great potential during his years at the academy.
