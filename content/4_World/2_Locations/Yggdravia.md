# Yggdravia
## Profile

**Description:** 
Yggdravia is the eastern of the Three Kingdoms, divided between a thin ground realm and its skyborne heart. Long ago the land beneath the stone-giant capital, [[Drasilgard]], tore free and rose above the clouds, severing roads and trade. Only [[Tollvarn]] remains below as a functioning settlement on the western border. The roads are poor, and the old Tally Way now ends at a broken arch outside Tollvarn. Rumors persist that a few have found ways to reach the sky-islands and speak with the giants, but no account is verified. Steward [[Sigurd Eisenfuß]] governs the ground realm in trust until contact with Drasilgard is restored.

**Heraldry & Faith**  
The emblem shows Yggdrasil, the world-tree, centered within a ring of towers that represent Drasilgard. Ground communities maintain simple shrines to the World-Tree, keep drift records of the sky-islands, and honor stonecraft and oath-keeping—customs inherited from the giants.

**Notable Settlements**

- [[Drasilgard]] (Capital)
- [[Tollvarn]] 

Yggdravia stands between earth and sky—enduring, practical, and patient—its Yggdrasil emblem a pledge that the path upward will one day be found and kept.
