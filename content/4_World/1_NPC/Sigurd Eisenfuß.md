# Sigurd Eisenfuß
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Sigurd Eisenfuß is a colossal stone giant whose sheer bulk makes even other giants seem measured in comparison. His body is a battlefield of history—crisscrossed with deep scars from countless wars, alongside faintly glowing runic tattoos that mark victories, oaths, and old burdens. He is completely bald, but wears a long, thick braided beard of grey and brown stone-like hair that hangs heavily over his chest. One side of his face is permanently marred by a brutal scar, and his missing eye gives him a constant, unsettling stare that never seems to fully rest. Where his left foot once was, there is now a brutal replacement of solid iron, forged and riveted directly to his body; each step he takes echoes like a hammer striking stone, often announcing his presence long before he is seen.

He wields a massive stone maul, worn smooth by years of crushing blows, and moves with the weight of a living siege engine.
### 1.2. Personality
Sigurd Eisenfuß is blunt, harsh, and dangerously straightforward. He hates his enemies with absolute certainty and tolerates his allies with reluctant loyalty. He does not waste time on strategy or philosophy unless forced to—he acts first, thinks later, and trusts instinct over planning. Despite this brutal exterior, he is not cruel for its own sake; beneath the violence lies a deeply rooted sense of duty to his people. He wants his folk to survive, endure, and remain strong in a world that constantly tries to break them.

His greatest frustration is his son, Yggvar, a brash and arrogant youth whom Sigurd cast out after too many reckless displays of pride. Yet even in anger, there is concern beneath it—Sigurd knows Yggvar is meant to inherit the mantle of Jarl, and quietly hopes exile will force him to grow into responsibility rather than ego.
