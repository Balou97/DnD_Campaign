## Profile

**Description:** 
Declan, a seasoned bounty hunter with ties to both the notorious [[The Guild]] and the shadowy [[Zhentarim]], has built a reputation for being cunning, resourceful, and lethal. His most infamous heist occurred when he and his companion, [[Alastor Moody]], attempted to steal the coveted [[Crystal Ball]] from [[The Deam]]. While Declan successfully secured the orb, the mission went awry when Alastor was brutally beaten and captured in the process. Declan managed to escape, but the failure weighed heavily on him, and he was forced to lay low for a while. Sometime later, Declan crossed paths with The Deam once more. This time, the encounter was far more brutal, culminating in a vicious battle that seemed to claim Declan's life.
