# Alastor Moody
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Alastor “Mad-Eye” Moody is a grizzled, battle-hardened wizard whose face is marked by deep scars, one of his eyes missing and replaced by a magical, ever-scanning blue eye that swivels independently. His hair is long, unkempt, and streaked with gray, and his leather coat is worn and patched from years of travel and combat. Inside the coat, he keeps a carefully curated collection of wands, most of which he obtained after defeating other powerful wizards; each wand carries unique effects and abilities, ready for use when needed. Every aspect of his appearance hints at a life lived constantly on edge, always prepared for danger.
### 1.2. Personality
Gruff, paranoid, and relentless, Moody trusts very few and constantly anticipates threats from all directions. He is fiercely loyal to his allies, highly strategic, and driven by a sense of duty to bring criminals to justice. While his blunt demeanor and constant vigilance can be intimidating, they are the result of years spent surviving deadly encounters and mastering the art of staying one step ahead of his enemies.
## 2. Biography
### 2.1. Background
Alastor “Mad-Eye” Moody, a veteran bounty hunter and respected member of [[The Guild]], has earned a reputation for his relentless determination and unmatched skill in tracking dangerous targets. His most infamous mission occurred alongside his partner [[Declan]], when they attempted to reclaim the coveted [[Crystal Ball]] from its current holders. While Declan managed to evade capture, Alastor was brutally overpowered and imprisoned, his strength and cunning no match for their foes in that encounter. His time in the notorious [[Waterdeep]] prison tested every ounce of his magical ability and mental resilience, leaving him more cautious and hardened than ever.
