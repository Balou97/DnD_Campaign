# Baron Revilgaz
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Baron Revilgaz stands taller and broader than most goblins, his presence commanding immediate attention. He is always impeccably dressed in a lavish purple suit trimmed with gold accents, a symbol of both his wealth and vanity. A tall, dark zylinder hat crowns his sharp-featured head, shadowing his piercing yellow eyes that gleam with cunning. His green hands are adorned with a glittering assortment of jeweled rings, each reflecting his taste for excess and power. Rarely seen without a glass of fine liquor in hand, he exudes an air of refined decadence—part gentleman, part menace.
### 1.2. Personality
Clever, calculating, and unapologetically ambitious, Revilgaz is the quintessential goblin mastermind. Every word he speaks drips with charm and manipulation, concealing razor-sharp instincts honed by years of underworld dealings. He thrives on control and profit, always seeking the next lucrative opportunity—no matter how dangerous or immoral. Beneath his polished demeanor lies a ruthless streak; those who cross him seldom get a second chance. To allies, he is persuasive and charismatic; to enemies, he is a patient and unforgiving predator.
## 2. Background
Born in the grimy depths of Kezan’s industrial slums, Revilgaz learned early that wit and guile were the true currencies of survival. A sharp mind and sharper tongue carried him from the gutters to the gambling dens, where he carved his first fortune through deceit, deals, and a few well-placed daggers. His rise through the ranks of the Bilgewater Cartel was as swift as it was bloody, earning him the title of Baron—a name he wears with pride and irony alike.

When the Cataclysm shattered old trade routes, Revilgaz saw opportunity where others saw ruin. Relocating his operations to the undercity of [[Oribos]], he rebuilt the cartel from the shadows, transforming it into a sprawling empire of smuggling, extortion, and arms dealing. His most profitable enterprise came from a pact with the infamous Strahd von Zarovich, trading forbidden weaponry supplied by a mysterious masked broker from [[Waterdeep]]. But profit alone could not sate his ambition. Now, with enslaved laborers digging an enormous tunnel between Oribos and Waterdeep, Baron Revilgaz seeks to cement his dominion across worlds—ensuring that wherever gold flows, his hand is the one that collects it.
