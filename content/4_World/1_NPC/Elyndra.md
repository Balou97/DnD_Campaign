## Profile

**Description:** 
Elyndra, an ancient red dragon of immense wisdom and power, has lived for thousands of years, seeing the rise and fall of civilizations across her long life. For much of that time, like many of her kind, she chose to live in isolation, relishing her solitude and dominion over her lands. But everything changed when she fell in love with a humanoid half-elf, an unexpected romance that brought both joy and complexity into her life. Together, they had children, but the pull of her draconic nature proved too strong. Elyndra realized that this life—one built on love and family—was not meant for her, and she left, torn by the decision but knowing it was necessary. Yet, the experience awakened something in her: a desire for companionship. She no longer wanted to live alone, though finding meaningful connections as a dragon was a challenge like no other.

After years of searching for a new sense of belonging, Elyndra found an unlikely bond with a clan of Githyanki warriors, who are famed for riding red dragons into battle. With them, Elyndra found a kinship she had never expected. Their fierce warrior spirit resonated with her own, and over time, she became one of them, joining their ranks and fighting alongside them in countless battles. The Githyanki branded her as one of their own—a mark of respect and honor that few dragons ever receive. For years, Elyndra thrived with the Githyanki, enjoying their companionship and the thrill of shared battle.

But her fate took a dark turn during a disastrous mission that led to their defeat. Captured and [[Female Dragon.jpeg|imprisoned in a dungeon]] for decades, Elyndra endured the cruel experiments of [[Dr. Dr. Jarvis]], a scientist from [[Springwater]]. He sought to extract the magical properties of her ancient dragon blood for his twisted experiments, leaving Elyndra drained and trapped, a shadow of her former glory. Though her body remains bound, her spirit remains unbroken, and deep inside, she harbors a fierce, burning desire for revenge against the one who dares to exploit her power.
