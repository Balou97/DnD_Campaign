## Profile

**Description:** 
Cad Bane, a ruthless lizardfolk gunslinger, is a high-ranking member of the notorious bounty hunter guild known as [[The Guild]]. He has made a name for himself as a cunning and cold-hearted manipulator, and his current residence on [[Cinderpeak Island]] reflects his dark ambitions. Years ago, Bane deceived the native lizardfolk into believing he was their savior, rescuing them from a supposed threat. However, his true intentions were far more sinister—he sold them into slavery to a tribe of orcs, forcing the lizardfolk to mine the valuable volcanic glass known as [[VolcanicGlass.jpg|Obsidian]] for the orcs' gain. As the self-appointed "tyrant" of the island, Bane rules with an iron fist, using both his deadly skill with firearms and his network of influence within "The Guild" to keep his hold on the island unchallenged.
