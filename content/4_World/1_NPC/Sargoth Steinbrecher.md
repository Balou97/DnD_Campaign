## Profile

**Description:** 
Sargoth Steinbrecher was once a proud member of a nomadic Goliath tribe known for their unyielding strength and deep connection to the mountains. From an early age, he was trained in the ways of his ancestors, mastering the arts of hunting, climbing, and surviving in the harshest conditions. His tribe revered the spirit of the mountains, and Sargoth found solace among the towering peaks, where the winds whispered secrets only the strong could hear. But that peace was shattered when ruthless mercenaries raided their camp under the cover of night. In the chaos, Sargoth was overpowered, shackled, and torn from the only life he had ever known. Stripped of his freedom, he was sold to the highest bidder like a beast of burden.

Sargoth's new existence was one of endless toil in the infernal depths of Dis, the second layer of the Nine Hells. The heat scorched his skin, and the air was thick with the acrid stench of sulfur. Here, his captors forced him into backbreaking labor, exploiting his immense strength to move heavy loads and perform grueling tasks for their twisted amusement. Yet, even in this hellish prison, Sargoth refused to let his spirit break. Each night, as he lay on the cold, rough ground, his thoughts drifted to the snow-covered peaks of his homeland and the crisp, clean air that once filled his lungs. He vowed silently that his story would not end in the depths of this infernal plane.

It was in this pit of despair that he met Garona, a fierce half-orc warrior who shared his unyielding determination. In her, Sargoth saw a reflection of his own indomitable spirit—a soul unwilling to submit despite the chains that bound them. Together, they formed an unspoken pact to endure, to resist, and one day, to break free of their infernal captors. For the first time since his capture, Sargoth felt the ember of hope stir within him, fanned by the thought that, perhaps, they could defy their fates and reclaim their freedom together.

## Traits

**Powerful Build**  
Sargoth counts as one size larger when determining his carrying capacity and the weight he can push, drag, or lift.

**Stone’s Endurance (1/Day)**  
As a reaction, Sargoth can reduce damage dealt to him by 1d12 + 2.

**Rage (3/Day)**  
As a bonus action, Sargoth can enter a rage for up to 1 minute. While raging, he gains the following benefits:
- Advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.
- +2 bonus to melee damage with Strength-based attacks.
- Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

## Actions

**Greataxe Slash**  
_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (1d12 + 4) slashing damage. If Sargoth is raging, he deals an additional 2 damage.

**Reckless Attack**  
On his turn, Sargoth can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against him have advantage until the start of his next turn.

**Throwing Boulder**  
_Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 15 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

## Reactions

**Defensive Slam**  
When a creature within 5 feet of Sargoth hits him with a melee attack, he can use his reaction to make a Greataxe Slash attack against that creature.
