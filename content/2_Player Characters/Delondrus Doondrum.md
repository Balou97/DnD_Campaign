# Delondrus Doondrum
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
A tall and imposing Loxodon Druid with white-gray skin and a calm, deliberate demeanor. He wears a simple brown robe, adorned with woven leaves and natural trinkets that drape gently over his shoulders. His wooden staff, carved with flowing natural patterns, serves as both a walking aid and a conduit of his druidic power. Despite his size, his movements are measured and unhurried, reflecting the patience and balance of the natural world he reveres.
### 1.2. Personality
Guided by a deep respect for life and the natural order, he views the world through the lens of nature’s quiet wisdom. He believes that preserving life and harmony is a sacred duty, seeing every living being as a part of the same vast cycle. Trusting by nature, he assumes goodwill in others until proven otherwise. His manner is calm and contemplative, often expressing thoughts through simple but profound metaphors drawn from nature. He carries with him a quiet reverence for the living world, symbolized by a cherished leaf from Vitu-Ghazi, which changes color with the seasons—a reminder of life’s resilience and constant renewal.
## 2. Background
Born beneath the whispering canopies of the High Forest, Delondrus Doondrum was raised among a secluded circle of druids who revered balance above all else. From a young age, his gentle strength and innate connection to the rhythms of the earth set him apart. He learned the old ways of the Circle of the Oakheart—guardians who healed the land and guided lost souls back to harmony. When greed and fire threatened his grove, Delondrus stood as both protector and peacemaker, wielding nature’s fury to defend its peace. That trial tempered his spirit, teaching him that even gentleness must sometimes wear the armor of resolve.

In his later travels, Delondrus crossed paths with [[Kahani]], a soul shaped by hardship and shadow, whose path often skirted the edge of the law. Where others saw only a criminal, Delondrus saw a wounded spirit—one still capable of growth and renewal. With quiet patience, he guided Kahani toward redemption, showing him that even roots twisted by darkness can still find their way to the light.
