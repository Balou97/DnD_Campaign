# Hrodvar Thorsgard
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Hrodvar Thorsgard is a towering stone giant whose body looks as though it was carved directly from storm-worn granite. Deep rune-scars glow faintly beneath his grey, chiseled skin, pulsing like embers trapped in stone. His hair is kept short and tightly bound into heavy braids, while his thick beard is split into weighted strands threaded with iron rings etched in ancient symbols. His massive arms are covered in old chisel marks and ritual cuts, each one a record of past work and pain endured in the name of his craft. He wears a reinforced smith’s apron over his frame, layered with steel plates and lined with rune-etched tools that hum softly with contained magic.
### 1.2. Personality
Hrodvar is stern, disciplined, and intensely focused, speaking little and wasting no words. He treats rune-carving as both sacred craft and solemn burden, believing that magic etched into flesh binds the fate of both smith and bearer. Though he appears cold or intimidating, he is deeply principled and takes personal responsibility for every rune he carves, as if each one ties the recipient’s survival to his own honor. Foolishness earns his silence or dismissal, but courage, endurance, and acceptance of pain earn his respect. He does not see himself as an artisan—but as someone who reshapes destiny, one scarred rune at a time.
## 2. Biography
### 2.1. Background
Born in the rune-forges of Drasilgard, capital of Yggdravia, Hrodvar Thorsgard was once counted among the most gifted rune-smiths of his generation. He studied under master carvers who etched history into stone, but Hrodvar believed runes were not meant to remain static monuments—they were meant to live, breathe, and fight. His experiments with carving runes into living flesh were condemned by the traditional forge-councils, especially after one of his marked subjects survived a battle that should have killed them, changed in ways neither fully understood nor controlled. Whether exiled or self-banished depends on who tells the story, but Hrodvar left Drasilgard and took his craft to the frontier of giant society. Now he offers his forbidden art openly, forging living runes into those strong enough to endure them, convinced that true strength is not carved in stone—but carried in flesh.
