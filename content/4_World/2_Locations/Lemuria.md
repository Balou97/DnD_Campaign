# Lemuria
## Profile

**Description:** 
Lemuria is the westernmost of the Three Kingdoms—a realm that weathered calamity and came out stronger. Once ruled from [[Springwater]], the kingdom shifted its seat after the Incident with [[Zephyr Solvyrth]]; the royal court and crown now reside in [[Waterdeep]]. House Dragonheart leads as the royal house, with [[Aurelion Dragonheart]] upon the throne. Under their steady rule, Lemuria has flourished: trade lanes hum, fields and workshops expand, and neighboring holdings have sworn fealty to share in the kingdom’s rising prosperity.

**Heraldry & Faith**  
The emblem of Lemuria bears Bahamut, the silver dragon, whose ideals—justice, protection, honor, and mercy—are carried by the good folk of the realm. House Dragonheart was elected to the kingshouse for embodying the will of Bahamut in their hearts, and their governance hews to those tenets: fair courts, shielded roads, and oaths that bind ruler and subject alike.

Lemuria stands in an age of expansion—proud, rebuilding, and watchful—its silver-dragon standard a vow that prosperity will be kept and defended.
