# Aurelion Dragonheart
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Aurelion Dragonheart is a distinguished half-elf with middle-length, fine brown hair showing early signs of aging on the sides of the head, near the forehead. His face carries the elegant features of his elven heritage softened by the warmth and humility of his human side. He often wears a rich red royal robe, embroidered with golden thread and symbols of Bahamut, befitting his status as King. Despite his regal attire, his bearing remains approachable, reflecting the humility and sense of duty that define his rule.
### 1.2. Personality
Aurelion is a paragon of duty, humility, and justice. He rules with a measured hand, valuing fairness and the well-being of his people above all else. Steadfast and reliable, he places family and honor above personal ambition. Though crowned king, he retains a sense of groundedness, preferring practical solutions over displays of opulence. He is deeply devoted to Bahamut, guiding his decisions with faith and moral clarity, and inspires loyalty through example rather than fear or intimidation.
## 2. Background
Born into the modest yet proud House Dragonheart of Waterdeep, Aurelion grew up with a strong sense of duty and reverence for Bahamut. His early life was defined by service, humility, and loyalty to his family. When an attack on Waterdeep destroyed the Dragonheart estate, Aurelion became the family's anchor, keeping them united through poverty and uncertainty.
After the assassination of King Arcturus XIII and the turmoil that followed, Lemuria sought a new ruler. Aurelion's steadfastness, loyalty, and dedication to justice made him the people's choice. Following the defeat of Zephyr, the Purple Dragon, Aurelion was crowned King of Lemuria, and the capital was moved from [[Springwater]] to [[Waterdeep]]. Under his reign, he has focused on stabilizing the kingdom, securing a prosperous future for his family, and ensuring the legacy of House Dragonheart endures.
