# Fend
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
FEND is a towering figure of polished steel and brass, standing well over most humanoids with the silhouette of a knight encased in heavy plate armor. His design is intricate yet functional, each piece of metal perfectly interlocked with the next, engraved faintly with sigils from the artificer who gave him life. When he moves, the quiet hum of internal gears can be heard beneath the weight of his steps. Though he carries no weapon, his massive, reinforced gauntlets bear the marks of countless hours spent crafting—and defending. At his side often scurries [[Alan.png|ALAN]], a small, cleverly built construct with whirring eyes and an inquisitive chirp, a reflection of FEND’s gentle craftsmanship.
### 1.2. Personality
Despite his imposing form, FEND’s demeanor is soft-spoken and kind, his deep, resonant voice carrying warmth rather than threat. He approaches others with open curiosity and an almost childlike faith in their goodness, often taking words and gestures at face value. His lack of understanding of subtle social cues can make interactions awkward, but his sincerity quickly wins people over. Beneath his calm exterior lies a fierce protector—when his friends are in danger, the gentle hum of his inner mechanisms turns into the roar of a storm, revealing the warrior he was built to be.
## 2. Background
FEND was built for the sole purpose of war. The kingdom that created him was overthrown before he could ever be activated. Afterward, he was sold to an artificer named [[Thereon Aelnor]] who managed to bring him to life and intended to use him as a lab assistant. Over the years, the two formed a strong bond—FEND became the artificer’s pupil, and the artificer became something like a father to him. One night, however, the artificer vanished without a trace. FEND waited for his return, but after several months with no sign of him, he set out on an adventure to find his teacher and uncover what truly happened that night.
