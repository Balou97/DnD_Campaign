# `=this.file.name`
## Profile

**Description:** 
The Amulet of the Devout for **Selûne** glimmers with moonlit elegance. Its silver chain holds a crescent-shaped pendant, polished to a radiant sheen, with the curve of the moon inlaid with pale sapphires that sparkle like starlight. Tiny flecks of mother-of-pearl trace the inner edge of the crescent, catching the light with every movement. At its center rests a small, glowing opal that seems to pulse gently, like the phases of the moon, giving the amulet an ethereal, otherworldly aura that speaks to Selûne’s divine grace and guidance.

## Powers:
This amulet bears the symbol of a deity inlaid with precious stones or metals. While you wear the holy symbol, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and the saving throw DCs of your spells.

While you wear this amulet, you can use your Channel Divinity feature without expending one of the feature's uses. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
