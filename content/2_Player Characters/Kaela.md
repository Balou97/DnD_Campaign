# Kaela
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Kaela is an Eladrin elf whose presence blends lethal elegance with raw intensity. Her hair is a striking mix of deep red and streaks of pale blonde, a living testament to both the fiery rage and icy control that define her. Her muscular, athletic build reflects years of rigorous monk training and the unnatural resilience granted by her transformation.

She wears traditional monk garb, tattered from battles past, accented by blood-red wristwraps that faintly glow when her Ki or the power of the Gauntlets of the Sanguine Soul stirs within her. Every movement is precise and deliberate, her body a weapon honed to strike silently. When she fights, her crimson aura flares, marking the moment she shifts from disciplined assassin to predatory hybrid of shadow and blood.
### 1.2. Personality
Kaela is disciplined, controlled, and patient—until provoked. Her training as a warrior of shadow taught her stealth, patience, and silent vigilance, but the trauma of her past and the hunger of her Gauntlets of the Sanguine Soul add a simmering intensity to her nature. She is calculated and focused, yet beneath the surface burns an unrelenting, predatory rage.

She believes in balance, acting as a guardian of the shadows, but her morality is shaped by survival and vengeance. Kaela values precision and control, but when the fight demands it, she allows the blood and bestial instincts within her to guide her strikes, making her a terrifying opponent who moves between shadowed assassin and relentless predator with deadly grace.
## 2. Biography
### 2.1. Background
Kaela was born in the Feywild, in a hidden enclave of Eladrin, and trained at the Monastery of the Wandering Dusk as a Warrior of Shadow. As a Spring Eladrin, she learned to walk silently through darkness, protecting her realm and maintaining balance with invisible precision. Her life was one of discipline, patience, and absolute control over both body and shadow.

Her world shattered when a pack of ancient, corrupted Fey-lycanthropes overran her monastery. Despite the monks’ mastery of stealth and subterfuge, Kaela witnessed the slaughter of her mentors and was forced to flee into the deepest, lightless archives. There she discovered the Gauntlets of the Sanguine Soul and completed a forbidden blood-ritual, consuming the Hunter’s Bane to survive and master the lycanthropy that had sought to consume her.

The ritual saved her mind but forever altered her essence. Her Eladrin nature, once fluid with the seasons, froze into a permanent reflection of trauma: the pale streaks in her hair represent the cold, disciplined control of Winter, while the deep red reflects the burning, predatory rage of Autumn. Now, she walks the material plane as a silent killer, blending shadow, blood magic, and lycanthropic strength. Kaela’s combat style merges the still, precise strikes of a monk with the brutal, unleashed ferocity of a predator, her Crimson Rite igniting whenever she channels the Gauntlets’ power. Driven by vengeance and the memory of her fallen order, she is a living embodiment of shadow, blood, and wrath.
