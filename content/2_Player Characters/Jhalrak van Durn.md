# Jhalrak van Durn
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Tall and pale, Jhalrak carries the poise of nobility even when caked in the dirt of travel. His frame is muscular and disciplined, a warrior’s body tempered by years of training. He dresses in an unsettling blend of fine noble garments and practical leather armor — elegance wrapped around danger. His quarterstaff, carved with strange sigils that pulse faintly in dim light, serves as both weapon and conduit. When his magic flares, the air around him hums, and shadows seem to stretch toward him as if in reverence.
### 1.2. Personality
Jhalrak is coldly composed — a man of intellect and ambition whose charm is deliberate and unsettling. He speaks with the confidence of one accustomed to command, yet there’s a quiet hunger in his gaze that betrays his inner corruption. His sense of morality is… fluid, shaped by the belief that power is the ultimate truth. Though he can act with honor and reason, those who linger too close soon notice the faint chill of something unnatural — for Jhalrak no longer feeds on food or wine, but on the essence of souls.
## 2. Background
Jhalrak van Durn was born into a lineage of proud aristocrats, raised among marble halls and ancient family portraits that whispered of honor and conquest. From a young age, he was drilled in the art of combat — discipline, precision, and control defining his every movement. Yet, Jhalrak’s curiosity was never satisfied by swordplay alone. Drawn to the mysteries of the arcane, he joined a prestigious magical academy, where his experiments soon crossed ethical lines. His obsession with binding life and power led him to forbidden pacts — and when the truth surfaced, he was cast out, his name stricken from the family’s record.
