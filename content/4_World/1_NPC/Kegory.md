# Kegory
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Kegory is an animated Alchemy-jug and also functions as one. He can pur all kinds of liquids out of his tubes, while he smiles with his cartoony eyes and mouth.
### 1.2. Personality
Kegory has a very inviting personality and instantly trusts even the oddest strangers that visit his little pub.
## 2. Biography
### 2.1. Background
62 years ago when the [[Knight Club]] was owned by someone else, a young and powerful wizard called [[Alatar]] visited the pub on a regular bases and to impress the town girls he very often used his magic powers for it. One night he used a wish spell to give the alchemy jug in the bar a soul and after the passing of the pub owner, Kegory took over.
