# Arvid
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Arvid is a stout, broad-shouldered dwarf whose presence commands respect both in battle and around the hearth. His thick red beard, braided and tied with iron rings, gleams like embers beneath his helm. Every line and dent in his weathered plate armor tells a story of battles fought and survived. Upon his arm he bears a sturdy shield engraved with the emblem of the Thunderblades, and in his hand rests his trusted warhammer — the symbol of his faith and his unyielding will. Though his armor shows signs of wear, it is always well cared for, a testament to his discipline and pride as both a warrior and a follower of Moradin.
### 1.2. Personality
Arvid embodies the heart of dwarven tradition — steadfast, loyal, and brimming with hearty laughter. He enjoys a good mug of ale and a roaring fire after a long day’s march, yet when duty calls, his demeanor hardens like forged steel. Beneath his gruff humor lies a deep devotion to Moradin, whose teachings guide his every act of courage and compassion. In battle, Arvid channels the divine might of his god to protect his allies and smite their foes, serving as both shield and hammer to those who stand with him. His wisdom and resolve make him a natural anchor for his companions — a foundation upon which their fellowship stands firm.
## 2. Biography
### 2.1. Background
Arvid is a seasoned adventurer of the Thunderblades. Together with [[Alden]] and [[Toruk]], he helped slay [[Gorthok the Thunder Boar.jpg|Gorthok the Thunder Boar]] near [[Amphail]]. Despite their victory, the group suffered the tragic loss of their companion Lyra during a battle against the Frost Wolves. After this ordeal, the Thunderblades journeyed north to [[Steelheim]], where they now rest and prepare for the next great adventure that awaits them.
