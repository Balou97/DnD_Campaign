## Profile

**Description:** 
Sproggie is a wiry, jittery goblin with a slightly wild look in his eyes, and his thin frame makes him seem even smaller than most. A few years older than his partner Krunk, Sproggie’s age shows in his scrappy appearance and the way he mutters to himself, often getting lost in his own thoughts. Known for his quick, sometimes erratic thinking, he’s as unpredictable as he is clever—a quality that’s both helped and complicated his life in Oribos.

Like Krunk, Sproggie was a member of the Bilgewatercartel, running schemes and scouting opportunities in Oribos until he crossed paths with "The Deam." He and Krunk didn’t stand a chance, and when they were defeated, Sproggie didn’t hesitate to join up with Jastor Gallywix’s Venture Trading Company, seeing it as just the next step up in his own strange journey. Now, with the Venture Trading Company in charge, Sproggie still works the streets of Oribos, playing the part of an eccentric but sharp-eyed scout. Though he might seem a bit off, Sproggie’s loyalty to Krunk and the Company is solid, and he brings a unique edge to the rough world of goblin business.

## Traits
**Nimble Escape**  
Sproggie can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of his turns.

**Unpredictable**  
When Sproggie makes a Dexterity saving throw to avoid damage, he can roll a d4 and add the result to the saving throw. 

**Tinker’s Wit**  
Sproggie has advantage on Intelligence checks to disable traps or discern the function of unfamiliar devices or objects.

## Actions
**Shortbow**  
_Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.

**Explosive Tinkerbolt (Recharge 5–6)**  
Sproggie fires a small, rigged bolt that explodes on impact. Each creature within 5 feet of the target must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

## Reactions
**Evasive Scurry**
