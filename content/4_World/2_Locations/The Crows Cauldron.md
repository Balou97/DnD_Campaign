## Profile

**Description:** 
Welcome to “The Crow’s Cauldron,” a giant-scale apothecary tucked along the broad avenues of [[Drasilgard]], capital of [[Yggdravia]]. Its wooden facade is plain but well kept, marked only by a wrought-iron sign of a raven perched over a simple pot. Lanterns cast a steady amber light across neat rows of phials, stoppered jars, and rune-labeled crates. The air smells of pine smoke, boiled herbs, and clean iron.

The shop carries tonics, tinctures, salves, scrolls, enchanted Items and much more. Nothing is flashy: ingredients are listed in a careful hand, dosages are scratched into slate tags, and every bottle bears a small raven mark for authenticity. A central hearth warms a pair of kettles; inventory is stored in cool alcoves; a standing-stone ledger records orders for the city watch, ship-crews, and scholars alike. Two tame crows—Ash and Soot—keep quiet watch from a beam above the till.

Presiding over it all is [[Bergmir Björnson]], a stone giant with a long, copper-ringed beard and a generous belly, known for calm manners and a habit of “quality control” sips. His voice is low and even; his humor is dry. He measures by oath before coin, waters down nothing without saying so, and prefers remedies that work over those that impress. If you need a clear head for council, a steady hand for rope work, or a night’s rest after rough seas, The Crow’s Cauldron provides it—plain, reliable, and fairly weighed.
