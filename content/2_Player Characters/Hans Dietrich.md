# Hans Dietrich
## 1. Description
### 1.1. Appearance
Hans Dietrich is a wiry old man with a long, unkempt beard the color of storm clouds and eyes that still glimmer with mischief beneath bushy brows. His robes are a mismatched collection of faded fabrics, patched and stained from years on the road, yet he wears them with the pride of a seasoned archmage. A crooked, wide-brimmed hat perpetually threatens to slide off his head, and the faint smell of pipe smoke and old parchment follows wherever he goes. His wagon, cluttered with scrolls, bottles, and trinkets of questionable authenticity, rattles along faithfully at his side.
### 1.2. Personality
Hans is a charming rogue of a wizard—clever, talkative, and never without a quick story or a sly grin. Though he built his life on trickery, there’s warmth in his deception; he prefers laughter to conflict and wit to violence. Deep down, he carries a tinge of regret for the years wasted chasing gold instead of wisdom, but he hides it well behind humor and bluster. When pressed, his sharp intellect and surprising flashes of genuine magical skill remind others that beneath the con man’s act lies a mind that once brushed greatness—and perhaps, with luck, might again.
## 2. Background
Hans Dietrich was once known across the trade roads as “Old Sparkfinger,” a traveling peddler of _miraculous scrolls_ and _enchanted charms_—most of which were little more than clever forgeries and sleight of hand. With a silver tongue and a twinkle in his eye, Hans roamed from town to town in his rickety wagon, selling fake spells to gullible farmers and wide-eyed adventurers. Yet beneath the showmanship and deception lay a genuine spark of arcane talent—one he had squandered long ago chasing coin instead of mastery.
